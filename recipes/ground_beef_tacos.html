Ground Beef Tacos
Ingredients

  • ground beef
  • taco seasoning 2
  • flour tortillas
  • tomatoes
  • sour cream
  • lettuce
  • shredded cheese
Steps

  • 1. throw meat in pan
  • 2. add two packets of taco seasoning
  • 3. add 1/4 cup water
  • 4. mix and cook well
  • 5. cut tomatoes
  • 6. rinse lettuce
