Atun
Ingredients

  • tuna cans (small) 4 cans
  • mayonnaise 7-8 spoons
  • carrot & peas mix 1 bag
  • tomatoes 2
Steps

  • 1. put carrots and peas to cook
  • 2. open tuna cans and drain juice
  • 3. add tuna to bowl
  • 4. add 7-8 spoons of mayonnaise and mix
  • 5. add in tomatoes and mix
  • 6. put in fridge to cool down
