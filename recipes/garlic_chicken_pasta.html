Garlic Chicken Pasta
Ingredients

  • spaghetti pasta 1/2 box
  • chicken 2
  • heavy whipping cream 2 cups
  • butter 6 tbsp
  • parmesan cheese 1 cup
  • salt
  • pepper
  • garlic powder
  • minced garlic
  • cayenne pepper
Steps

  • 1. put half a box of pasta to boil
  • 2. cut chicken into halves and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, cayenne pepper, and minced garlic
  • 3. add oil to pan and set to low-med heat
  • 4. add 6 tbsp of butter to another pan and let it melt
  • 5. once butter is melted add in the 2 cups of heavy whipping cream
  • 6. cook chicken in pan and set aside
  • 7. check pasta
  • 8. if whipping cream is boiling add in the 1 cup of parmesan cheese and mix it until it is fully melted
  • 9. once the cheese melts add in the pasta
  • 10. serve with the chicken
