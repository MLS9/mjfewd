Beef Broccoli
Ingredients

  • thin steak
  • broccoli
  • cooking wine 1 tbsp
  • salt 1/4 tsp
  • baking soda 1/4 tsp
  • cornstarch 2 tbsp
  • minced garlic 1 tbsp
  • ginger 1 tbsp

    • Sauce

  • light soy sauce 1/2 cup
  • water 1/2 cup
  • white sugar 3 tbsp
  • cornstarch 2 tbsp
  • sesame oil 1 tsp
Steps

  • 1. put rice to cook
  • 2. put broccoli to cook
  • 3. cut thin steak into strips and put in a bowl
  • 4. put pan on medium heat and add enough oil to cover the bottom
  • 5. add meat to bowl with steak pieces and mix together:
    - 3 tbsp water
    - 1 tbsp cooking wine
    - 1/4 tsp salt
    - 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 6. mix it all together and then add 2 tbsp cornstarch to meat and mix again
  • 7. add meat pieces to pan with oil and cook on both sides (they cook fast)
  • 8. set meat pieces aside in a bowl
  • 9. take out most of the oil from the pan and leave about 1 tbsp and set heat to low medium
  • 10. mix sauce in a bowl:
    - 1/2 cup of light soy sauce (not the regular it will be too salty)
    - 1/2 cup water
    - 3 tbsp white sugar
    - 2 tbsp cornstarch
    - 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 11. add 1 tbsp of minced garlic and 1 tbsp of ginger to pan with the oil and mix until it's yellowish brown
  • 12. make sure sauce in bowl is mixed in and add it in and continue mixing until it starts to thicken (put the heat up a little bit if it's not thickening)
  • 13. once it's thick add the steak pieces back in and the broccoli
